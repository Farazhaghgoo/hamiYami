Know more about every meal

Trust is central to what we’re building. Our planned approach includes:

  • Planned

    Clear cook profiles

    Each cook profile would state exactly which onboarding checks we’ve completed. An identity check confirms who a cook is — it isn’t a guarantee of food safety, and profiles will say so.

  • Planned

    Ingredients and allergens

    Ingredient and allergen information, as declared by the cook, available on every listing before you order.

  • Planned

    Clear collection details

    Pickup windows and collection instructions attached to each order, so you know when and where to go.

  • Planned

    Support and order-based reviews

    A way to raise an issue if something goes wrong, and to share feedback once an order has been completed.

These features are still being designed. We’ll share the details before the pilot opens.
